# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

## The short version

- Chores is for families. A parent sets it up; children never create an account.
- For a child, we only keep the nickname, picture and colour a parent chooses, and their chores and points.
- We don't show adverts, we don't track you, and we never sell or share your family's information.
- Only people in your family can see your family's information.
- You can delete everything at any time in the app.

**For children:** Chores keeps your nickname, your picture and the chores you tick off, so your family can see how you're doing. Only your family can see it. We don't ask for your real name, where you live, or anything else about you. If you're worried about anything, talk to a grown-up you trust.

## Who we are

Chores is made and run by Sam Morgan, an individual (sole trader) in the United Kingdom. I am the "controller" of the information described here; "we" in this policy means me.

Questions about privacy: chores@bantersmith.com

## What we collect, and why

**Parents**
- Your email address, so you can sign in with a code we email you. If you choose to use a password, it is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
- The name and picture you choose, shown to your family.

**Children (entered by a parent)**
- A nickname (a real name isn't needed), a picture chosen from the app's icons, and a colour.
- Settings a parent chooses, such as how many chores a day, weekly pocket money and reward prices.

**Your family's chores and rewards**
- Your family name and time zone; the chores, rewards and days off you set up; which child each chore is given to on which day, and when it's ticked off; points, pocket money and rewards; and reward ideas a child suggests. Reasons for days off and notes on pocket money are optional and only seen by your family.

**Your subscription**
- Apple handles payment, so we never see your card or bank details. When a parent subscribes, we keep the App Store's reference for the subscription, when it ends, whether it's in the free week and will renew, and which parent bought it. This is so one subscription can cover every phone in the family.

**Children's phones**
- When a child's phone is connected, we create an anonymous account for that phone. It has no email, name or phone number. We also store the type of device (for example "iPhone").

**Bug reports and ideas (only if you send one)**
- What you write, and your email address only if you turn on "You can email me back".
- If "Include diagnostics" is on: the app version, device model (for example "iPhone16,2"), iOS version, language, time offset from UTC, display settings, and a log of recent actions in the app (such as which screens were opened and whether requests to our server worked). The log never includes names, email addresses, chore or reward titles, or anything typed. IDs in it are replaced with random codes that can't be linked back to your family. You can see exactly what is included before you send it.

**What stays on your phone**
- Face ID is handled by iOS. We never see your face or fingerprint data.
- The camera is used only to scan the code that connects a child's phone. Pictures aren't saved or sent.
- Daily reminders are scheduled on the child's phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.
- The diagnostics log is kept on the phone for up to 3 days and is only sent if you send a report.

**What we don't collect:** no location, contacts, photos, birthdays, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking. There are no adverts and no third-party analytics in the app.

## Our legal reasons

- **To provide the app you asked for** (contract): your account, your family's chores, your subscription, and keeping your family's phones in sync.
- **Legitimate interests:** keeping the app secure (for example limiting how often codes can be guessed) and fixing problems you report.
- **Consent:** sharing your email address with a bug report so we can reply. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers, who may only use your information to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase** stores the app's data and runs sign-in. Data is held in London, UK. Supabase, Inc. is based in the United States, so we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and its data processing terms to protect any access from outside the UK.
- **ElevenLabs** reads chores aloud when "Read chores aloud" is on. We send it only the chore's title (never who it's for), once per title; the audio is kept with the chore and deleted when the chore is. It is based in the United States and protected the same way.
- **Resend** delivers sign-in code emails and the bug reports you choose to send. It is based in the United States and protected the same way.
- **Apple** provides the App Store and takes subscription payments, as well as Face ID and (if you use it) Sign in with Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy. We ask Apple's App Store Server only about your family's subscription.

We never sell your information or share it for marketing. We will only share it with others if the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

- Your family's information is kept for as long as your account exists, so you can see past progress.
- Unused codes expire (15 minutes to connect a phone, 7 days to invite a parent) and are deleted soon after. Anonymous accounts on phones that never finished connecting are deleted after a day.
- Bug reports are kept in our inbox only as long as we need them to fix the problem, and no longer than 12 months.
- When you delete your account, your information is deleted straight away. Our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within 7 days.

## Keeping it safe

Everything is encrypted on the way to our servers (HTTPS) and while stored. Each family's information is walled off at the database level, so a phone can only ever read its own family's information. Codes for connecting phones and inviting parents can be used only once, expire, and are limited to stop guessing. Sign-in on your phone is kept in the iPhone's secure Keychain, and you can add a Face ID lock.

## Deleting your information

- **Delete your account:** in the app, go to **Family → Delete account**. If you're the only parent, this permanently deletes your family, all children's profiles, chores and history, and disconnects any children's phones. If there's another parent, only your own account is removed.
- **Remove a child:** **Family →** the child **→ ⚙︎ → Remove**. This deletes that child's profile and history.
- **Disconnect a child's phone:** from the child's settings in the parent app, or from the child's phone itself.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Email chores@bantersmith.com and we'll reply within a month. Parents can do this for their children. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Children's privacy

We follow the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code) and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Children's profiles are created and controlled by a parent, hold only a nickname and an icon, and are visible only to that family. Children can't contact anyone outside their family through the app, and the parts of a child's phone that leave the app or send us a message are behind a grown-up check.

## Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll tell you in the app.
