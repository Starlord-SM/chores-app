# Questions and answers

## Getting started

**How do I sign in?**
Tap **Continue with Apple**, or enter your email address and we'll email you a 6-digit code. Either way, the same step creates your account the first time. There's no password to remember.

**How does a second parent join?**
Go to **Family → Invite a parent** and share the code. They install Chores, sign in with their own email, choose **Join my partner's family** and enter the code within 7 days.

## Children

**Does my child need an account or an email address?**
No. A child's profile is just a nickname, a picture and a colour, which you choose.

**How do I connect my child's phone?**
On your phone, go to **Family →** your child **→ ⚙︎ → Set up …'s phone** (with your child's name). On your child's phone, install Chores, tap **Set up a child's phone** and scan the code (or type it in). Codes work once and expire after 15 minutes.

**My child doesn't have a phone.**
Use **Child Mode**: go to **Family → Start Child Mode on this phone** and hand it over. Your child taps their picture and ticks off their chores. Use Face ID to get back to the grown-up side.

**Can I stop my children ticking each other's chores in Child Mode?**
Yes. Go to **Family →** your child **→ ⚙︎ → Set a PIN** and choose four numbers. In Child Mode they type it after tapping their picture, and again whenever Chores has been closed. A child's own phone never asks for it. If they forget it, change it in the same place.

**Can I be told when the chores are done?**
Yes. Go to **Family → Notifications** and turn them on. You can hear as soon as a child ticks off their last chore of the day, and get an evening summary at a time you choose. Each parent chooses for their own phone. On a child's phone, the daily reminder (in its settings) only arrives if chores are left.

**What happens with no signal?**
Ticks are saved on the phone and sent as soon as it's back online.

## Chores, points and rewards

**How are chores shared out?**
Each child gets their daily number of chores. You can pick them all, let the app pick, or mix the two. The app keeps things fair: household chores go to one child a day, and nobody gets the same chore two days running if it can be helped.

**How do points and pocket money work?**
Ticking off chores earns points, which children can spend in your rewards shop. Rewards are priced in weeks of chores ("half a week", "1 week", "a month"), and each child pays what that much of their own chores earns them, so every child can save at the same pace and every chore counts. Weekly pocket money is shared out by how many of the week's chores were done, and added at the start of each week: Sunday, unless you choose another day in **Family settings**. You decide every amount, and you approve every reward.

**How do badges work?**
There are over 100 badges to collect: for chores done, streaks, perfect days, weeks, months and even a perfect year, early mornings, trying new chores and special days like Christmas. To add bonus points to a badge, open a child's **Rewards**, tap the badge and choose an amount. Each child who earns it from then on gets the bonus once.

**What are extra jobs?**
Jobs children can choose to do for bonus points, like washing the car. Add one in **Chores** with **+ → Extra job**. Once a child has ticked their chores for the day, they pick a job from the board and tap **I did it!**

**How do I cheer my child on?**
Open a child's **Plan** and tap the smiley next to a ticked chore. A cheer pops up on their phone. Children can also change their avatar by tapping their picture, and earning badges unlocks special avatars.

**Who reads the chores aloud?**
Tap **Read it** on a chore to hear it. The voice is made by ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io). Without a signal, the iPhone's own voice reads it instead. Parents can turn it off in each child's settings.

## Subscription

**How much does Chores cost?**
The first week is free, then it's £1.99 a year, through the App Store. You can cancel any time up to 24 hours before it renews, in **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions**.

**Does every parent need to pay?**
No. One subscription covers the whole family: every parent's phone and every child's phone. Once one parent has subscribed, nobody else in the family is asked to.

**I changed phones and Chores is asking me to subscribe.**
Tap **Restore purchases**, signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

## Your data

**Who can see our information?**
Only people in your family. See the privacy policy for the details.

**How do I delete our data?**
Go to **Family → Delete account**. If you're the only parent, this deletes your whole family's information straight away.

## Getting help

**Something isn't working.**
Go to **Help → Report a problem**. Diagnostics help us find the problem quickly, and you can see exactly what's included before sending.

**I have an idea.**
Go to **Help → Suggest an idea**. We read every one.

**Contact us**
Email chores@bantersmith.com, for help and for privacy questions.
